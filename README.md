# Data-structure
The present work addresses the implementation of an extended version of the textual authentication process. This procedure is carried out through algorithms that ensure the correct identification of authors in electronically issued documents. This tool consists of a mechanism capable of guaranteeing the authenticity of the identity of the signatory of a document, which is essential to provide legal security in procedures of different natures, such as petitions, commercial transactions, and agreements.

In addition to ensuring that the file was generated by an authorized and properly identified person, digital authentication also confirms the origin and integrity of a given document. In this way, it is possible to verify whether any modification has occurred in the electronic document, no matter how small it may be, protecting it from possible interceptions that could compromise its content.

Finally, digital authentication is also a useful tool for authorizing the final readers of a document, since they will only have access to the file after going through an identity verification process. This characteristic is particularly useful because it allows not only the verification of the authenticity of the document's issuer, but also control over who will be the recipients of the sent file, maintaining the confidentiality of the information from unauthorized individuals.
